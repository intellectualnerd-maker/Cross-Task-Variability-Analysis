BASELINE VS. VARIABILITY COMPARISON EXPERIMENT
======================================================================

Timestamp: 2026-04-09 21:01:32
Dataset: DARWIN Handwriting Dataset
Samples: 174 (AD: 89, Healthy: 85)

HYPOTHESIS
--------------------------------------------------
Cross-task variability captures early cognitive instability
that is not reflected in mean task performance.

EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------
Baseline Model: 18 mean features only (*_mean)
Full Model: 90 features (mean + std + cv + iqr + range)
Classifier: Random Forest (300 trees, max_depth=10)
Validation: 5-fold stratified cross-validation

RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------
Baseline AUC: 0.9283 ± 0.0659
Full AUC:     0.9594 ± 0.0275
AUC Improvement: +0.0311 (+3.35%)

STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------------------
Paired t-test: t = 1.6886, p = 0.1666
Cohen's d: 0.7552 (medium effect)

CONCLUSION: No statistically significant improvement detected.
This may indicate insufficient sample size or that variability
information is partially captured by mean features.
